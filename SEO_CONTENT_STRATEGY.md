## 🎨 Content Writing Guidelines

### Keyword Density
- Primary keyword: 1-2% (natural placement)
- Secondary keywords: 0.5-1%
- LSI keywords: Throughout content

### Structure
- H1: One per page (include primary keyword)
- H2: Every 300-400 words
- H3: For subsections
- Paragraphs: 2-3 sentences max
- Lists: Use bullet points and numbered lists

### Internal Linking
- Link to 3-5 related pages in each article
- Use descriptive anchor text
- Link from new content to old content
- Link from old content to new content (update regularly)

### External Linking
- Link to authoritative sources (government sites, industry publications)
- 2-3 external links per article
- Open in new tab

### Call-to-Actions
- One CTA per 500 words
- Vary CTA text: "Get a Quote", "Contact Us", "Request Staff", "Learn More"
- Use buttons for primary CTAs

## 📸 Image Optimization

### Requirements for All Images
- Format: WebP (with JPG fallback)
- Size: < 200KB
- Dimensions: Appropriate for use case
- Alt text: Descriptive with keywords
- File names: descriptive-with-keywords.webp

### Alt Text Examples
**Bad:** "image1.jpg"
**Good:** "construction-workers-dubai-workora-manpower-supply.jpg"

**Bad:** "Construction workers"
**Good:** "Skilled construction workers provided by Workora Manpower Supply in Dubai, UAE"
